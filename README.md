# Amicon Cycles - Bicycle Retail Website

## Project Overview Part 1

Amicon Cycles is a professional, responsive website designed for a bicycle retail store based in Secunda, Mpumalanga. The website serves as a digital storefront, showcasing the store's products, services, and brand identity while providing customers with an easy way to enquire about products and services.

This project was developed as part of the Web Development (WEDE5020) POE at The Independent Institute of Education (The IIE). The design draws inspiration from the [Detour Bike Shop](https://detourcycles.co.za/) website, adapted to create a unique brand identity for Amicon Cycles.

## Website Goals and Objectives

### Primary Goals
1. Build Community: Provide valuable cycling information to engage customers
2. Brand Awareness: Showcase the product catalogue online to increase visibility
3. Lead Generation: Generate leads through service bookings and online enquiries
4. Increase Foot Traffic: Drive more customers to the physical store

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Increase website traffic by 25% within the first 3 months after launch
- Achieve 15 service bookings per month generated through the website
- Reach 150 visitors in-store for the first 6 months after launch

## Key Features and Functionality

### Page Structure

- Home:`index.html` | Welcome page with hero banner, featured products, services preview, and customer testimonials
- About: `about.html` | Store history, mission, vision, core values, and team information 
- Products: `products.html` | Product catalogue with categories (Mountain, Road, Electric, Kids, Accessories) 
- Services: `services.html` | Repair services, servicing packages, and custom builds 
- Enquiry: `enquiry.html` | Customer enquiry form with multiple fields and automatic email notifications 
- Contact: `contact.html` | Store location with Google Maps integration and contact details
- Sitemap: `sitemap.html` | Visual website structure and page hierarchy

## Navigation Structure
The website features a consistent navigation menu across all pages:
Home - About - Products - Services - Enquiry - Contact - Sitemap

### Technical Features
- Responsive Design: Mobile-first responsive design for all devices
- Google Maps Integration: Store location with embedded map
- Contact Forms: Enquiry forms with required fields and validation
- WhatsApp Integration: Instant communication for customers
- Consistent Branding: Grey, white, blue, and orange colour scheme

## Design and User Experience

### Colour Scheme

Grey: `#808080` | Primary brand colour 
White: `#FFFFFF` | Background and text
Blue: `#1A237E` | Accent colour, headers 
Orange: `#E64A19` | Highlight colour, buttons

### Typography
- Headings: Bold, strong fonts for impact
- Body Text: Open Sans (clean, readable, accessible)

### User Experience Considerations
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Clear call-to-action buttons for enquiries and ordering
- Clean, accessible layouts with intuitive navigation
- High-quality images central to user experience
