About Amicon Cycles

Your trusted bicycle retail store in Secunda, Mpumalanga. Quality bikes, expert servies, and a community that rides together

Our Story

Amicon Cycles was established in 2017 by Jaco Visser, a passionate cyclist and an experienced mechanic. What started as a small repair shop servicing bicycles for local mine workers and residents in Secunda has grown into a full-service bicycle retail destination.

Located in the heart of Secunda, Mpumalanga, our store offers an extensive selection of bicycles, accessories, and parts from our trusted brands. Our services range from routine maintenance and complex repairs to custom bike builds tailored to each rider's preferences.

At Amicon Cycles, we believe cycling is more than just a mode of transport - it's a lifestyle that promotes health, freedom, and community.
